# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and welcoming, assume good faith, give and accept constructive feedback, and focus on
what is best for the community. Harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks, and publishing
others' private information (including their voice without consent) are not acceptable.

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainers through a
[GitHub security advisory](https://github.com/chiarorosa/projeto-morpheu/security/advisories/new) or by
contacting the repository owner on GitHub. Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove
contributions and block participants who do not follow this code.
